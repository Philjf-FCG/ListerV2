I am writing to express my strong interest in the Head Game Scout position at Critical Reflex. My career has been defined by identifying high-potential development teams and brokering successful partnerships, skills I believe are perfectly aligned with Critical Reflex’s mission to build a high-impact portfolio.

For the past several years, my work has centred on the nexus between developers, studios, and publishers. My background, which includes serving as a Head of Operations for a studio and running my own operational services company, FoxChickenGrain, has provided me with a unique advantage: I don't just assess a pitch; I evaluate the operational health and long-term viability of the team behind it, a essential form of due diligence for any game scout.

Running FoxChickenGrain has been instrumental in cultivating a robust and trusted professional network, establishing me as a well-known and recognized figure across the industry. This is a critical asset for a Head Scout, as it translates directly into early access to top-tier talent and projects. My industry visibility, including appearances in Gamereactor and presenting at BCN Game Fest on the future of minority studios (links provided below), demonstrates a strong platform for attracting and vetting emerging talent.

My scouting taste is strategically diverse, allowing me to identify breakout hits across distinct market segments. This vision spans two high-growth extremes:

Niche Appeal (The Cozy Core): Spotting the next wave of accessible, high-retention titles like Babushcats, Mini Metro, and Is This Seat Taken? I am currently in regular contact with the developers of Babushcats to follow their pitching process closely.

Genre Mastery (Psychological Horror): Identifying projects with intense psychological depth and high production value, such as The Scourge, Supernatural, and Dead Frontier. I am currently leveraging my network to collaborate with a veteran studio director on an original horror concept.

The games I wish I had signed best illustrate my long-term market foresight:

Diablo (1990s): My reasoning is simple: the ability to recognize a genre-defining mechanical foundation that would sustain one of the industry's most successful and enduring franchises for decades.

Babushcats: This pick demonstrates my commitment to identifying emergent, high-demand trends and my active engagement with the development team long before the project is finalized.

South of Midnight: My selection here reflects a commitment to cultural foresight over immediate sales figures. This title represents the future of prestige IP: games that achieve profound, sympathetic portrayal of complex belief systems, setting a new benchmark for narrative integrity and meaningful representation within the industry. It’s a game that builds a brand’s reputation for quality and thematic depth.

I am confident that my strategic network, operational due diligence skills, and proven market foresight will allow me to immediately drive successful acquisitions for Critical Reflex. I look forward to discussing how my experience can directly benefit your portfolio growth.
